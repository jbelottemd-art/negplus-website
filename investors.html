Investment Thesis

Why NEG+ Innovations

Six structural advantages that differentiate NEG+ from typical pre-revenue medtech investments at the same stage. The addition of MUMS™ approximately doubles the platform TAM, lifts Year-5 blended gross margin to ~71%, and expands the strategic-acquirer set from six to eleven.

01 · Physician-Led

Clinical Problem–First Design

Every device in the portfolio emerged from documented gaps that Dr. Belotte encountered directly in women's health surgical practice — then validated through voice-of-customer research and engineering feasibility before a single patent was filed. No laboratory-first product-market fit risk.

02 · Portfolio Architecture

Four Devices. Two Acquirer Pools. One Exit Thesis.

MUMS™ (intrapartum) anchors a cross-life-stage platform alongside three gynecologic-surgery and pelvic-floor devices — spanning labor & delivery, MIGS, urogynecology, and pelvic pain. This creates two structurally distinct strategic-acquirer pools: maternal-fetal monitoring incumbents (Philips, GE HealthCare, Masimo, Edan, Nihon Kohden) and gyn-surgery and pelvic-floor majors (Medtronic, Olympus, Stryker, Hologic, BSC, CooperSurgical). Single-device divestiture optionality is preserved at every product level.

03 · Non-Dilutive Leverage

NIH SBIR/STTR as Equity Shield

NEG+ pursues NIH SBIR and STTR grants as the primary early-stage funding mechanism — providing peer-reviewed federal validation of core technology. Each grant preserved dilutes founder and investor equity by $0, while simultaneously strengthening diligence materials through independent scientific review.

04 · Founder Alignment

Skin-in-the-Game Co-Investment

Dr. Belotte participates personally as co-investor on identical economic terms alongside every outside investor — no separate founder economics, no preferred founder pricing. Capital conviction is demonstrated through cash, not just sweat equity. Full alignment from first dollar raised.

05 · Regulatory De-Risking

Industry-Grade Regulatory Expertise

The founder's role as Medical Director, Oncology Clinical Development at GlaxoSmithKline provides direct Phase I/II regulatory strategy expertise — transferred to NEG+'s FDA 510(k), De Novo, and IDE pathway design. Supported by a former FDA Principal Deputy Commissioner on the Advisory Board.

06 · Tax Advantage

QSBS — Up to 100% Federal Exclusion

NEG+ Innovations is a Delaware C-Corp structured for Qualified Small Business Stock eligibility under IRC §1202. Investors acquiring stock at current stage may qualify for up to 100% federal capital gains exclusion on qualifying gains — a material tax advantage unavailable at Series B and beyond.
