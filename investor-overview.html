Executive Snapshot

NEG+ Innovations at a Glance

NEG+ Innovations, Inc. is a physician-led medical device company advancing women's pelvic health through precision surgical and diagnostic innovation. Founded by Dr. Jimmy Belotte — an OB/GYN, gynecologic oncology clinical developer at GlaxoSmithKline, and reproductive physiologist — the company's four-device portfolio addresses documented, unmet clinical needs across four adjacent market segments: minimally invasive gynecologic surgery (MIGS), pelvic organ prolapse quantification, pelvic pain management, and intrapartum fetal monitoring. Each invention was conceived from first-hand surgical practice, validated through voice-of-customer research and engineering feasibility, and formalized into intellectual property prior to capital raise.

The company is structured as a Delaware C-Corporation with a parallel PLLC medical affiliate (NEG+ Innovations PLLC), providing full IP ownership, clean cap table, QSBS eligibility, and both strategic-acquirer pools simultaneously accessible. The founding team and advisory board encompass FDA regulatory strategy, clinical development, biomedical engineering, and capital markets expertise — covering every function required from pre-clinical through commercialization without reliance on outsourced scientific leadership.

NEG+ is currently raising its initial Seed round via SAFE instrument under Reg D Rule 506(b), with proceeds deployed toward prototype engineering, provisional-to-PCT patent conversion, NIH STTR/SBIR grant development, and regulatory strategy initiation. The founder participates as a co-investor on identical economic terms alongside every outside investor.

Company Summary

Opportunity, Products & Business Model

Opportunity

Women's pelvic health represents one of the most underserved and underinvested segments in surgical medicine. An estimated 1 in 3 women will experience a pelvic floor disorder in her lifetime, yet the diagnostic and surgical tools currently available to clinicians remain largely unchanged from instruments introduced decades ago. Intraoperative precision, objective quantification, and real-time monitoring are either absent or fragmented across disconnected platforms — creating avoidable surgical variability, extended procedural times, and suboptimal patient outcomes.

Existing solutions share a common failure: they were engineered without clinical operator input, leading to tools that prioritize manufacturing convenience over surgical utility. NEG+ addresses this gap by developing devices that emerged from the operating room itself — built on documented deficiencies that Dr. Belotte directly encountered and systematically validated before engineering began.

Market Segments

MIGS / Gyn Surgery

The global minimally invasive gynecology market is projected to grow substantially through 2030, driven by increasing hysterectomy volumes, adoption of robotic platforms, and demand for precision surgical tools. The Belotte Colpotomizer™ addresses a direct intraoperative bottleneck in laparoscopic and robotic hysterectomy.

Pelvic Floor / Urogynecology

Pelvic organ prolapse affects approximately 50% of parous women. Current POP-Q assessment is manual, examiner-dependent, and highly variable. POP-Q AutoSense™ introduces the first automated, standardized measurement platform — addressing a persistent clinical workflow gap.

Pelvic Pain / Regional Anesthesia

Pudendal nerve block is used across a broad range of gynecologic procedures and pelvic pain management. The market for precision regional anesthesia devices in OBGYN is underdeveloped relative to orthopedic counterparts — representing a significant whitespace opportunity.

Maternal-Fetal Monitoring

The global fetal monitoring market exceeds $3B and is growing at a high single-digit CAGR. MUMS™ targets an unmet need in intrapartum monitoring by providing multi-parameter, integrated uterine and fetal surveillance — a SaMD + hardware platform positioned for De Novo clearance.

Regulatory & Reimbursement Environment

The FDA has accelerated 510(k) and De Novo pathway timelines for women's health devices under recent policy initiatives addressing sex-based research gaps. CPT reimbursement codes exist for all clinical procedures targeted by NEG+'s portfolio — eliminating reimbursement pathway risk at the device level. NIH STTR/SBIR grant programs specifically fund small business medical device development in reproductive health, providing non-dilutive capital access aligned with NEG+'s pipeline.

Venture Financing in Women's Health MedTech

Women's health technology attracted over $1.8B in venture investment in 2024, with medical devices representing the fastest-growing subsector. Despite the scale of unmet need, femtech and women's health devices remain structurally underfunded relative to comparable male-health equivalents — creating a favorable entry environment for differentiated, physician-originated innovations with clear regulatory paths and large strategic acquirer bases.
